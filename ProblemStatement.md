
# Problem Statement — Climora

## 1. Problem Overview

Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) play an important role in economic growth and employment. However, businesses operating in climate-exposed regions face increasing risks from floods, heatwaves, storms, droughts, and water shortages.

These climate hazards can disrupt business operations, damage assets, interrupt supply chains, increase operating expenses, and reduce revenue. Such disruptions may also affect a business's ability to repay loans.

Financial institutions often rely heavily on conventional financial indicators and historical business performance when assessing credit risk. These indicators may not adequately capture a business's current climate exposure or vulnerability to climate-related disruptions.

As a result, two businesses with similar financial histories may have significantly different levels of climate-related risk.

## 2. Problem Statement

There is a need for a dynamic and explainable climate-adjusted financial risk assessment system that combines business information, location-based climate hazards, and financial indicators to help financial institutions better understand the climate-related vulnerabilities of MSMEs.

The system should identify relevant climate risks, explain their potential impact on business operations, and support timely risk assessment and early intervention.

## 3. Target Users

### Primary Users

**Banks and Lending Institutions**

* Assess climate-related risks before and during the lending relationship.
* Identify businesses exposed to significant climate hazards.
* Support more informed credit-risk reviews.
* Identify borrowers who may benefit from early intervention.

**Credit-Risk Analysts**

* Examine climate exposure alongside financial indicators.
* Understand the factors contributing to a business's risk assessment.
* Compare the relative climate vulnerabilities of businesses.

### Secondary Users

**MSME Owners**

* Understand the climate hazards that may affect their businesses.
* Identify operational vulnerabilities.
* Receive recommendations for preparedness and business continuity.

**Insurance Companies and Development-Finance Institutions**

* Gain additional insight into climate-related business exposure.
* Support risk monitoring and resilience planning.

## 4. Proposed Solution

Climora is a climate-intelligence platform designed to support climate-adjusted financial risk assessment for MSMEs.

The platform will collect basic business information, including location, industry, revenue, expenses, and selected operational dependencies. It will combine these details with available weather and climate indicators from external data sources.

Using a transparent prototype scoring methodology, Climora will generate an illustrative climate-adjusted risk score and explain the factors contributing to the result.

The platform will also provide risk indicators and practical recommendations to help users understand potential vulnerabilities and consider appropriate early-intervention measures.

## 5. Key Objectives

1. Integrate business information with available climate indicators.
2. Identify relevant climate hazards affecting MSMEs.
3. Develop an explainable prototype climate-adjusted risk score.
4. Present risk factors through an accessible dashboard.
5. Provide recommendations for climate preparedness and business continuity.
6. Support human decision-making without relying solely on traditional financial indicators.

## 6. Expected Outcomes

* Improved visibility into climate-related business vulnerabilities.
* Clearer explanations of climate factors that may affect financial resilience.
* Better-informed risk reviews by financial institutions.
* Identification of businesses that may require additional monitoring or preparedness support.
* Increased awareness of climate risks among MSME owners.

## 7. Project Scope

The initial prototype will focus on a limited set of business inputs and available climate indicators. It will demonstrate the end-to-end process of collecting business details, evaluating relevant indicators, generating an illustrative score, and presenting an explainable assessment.

Advanced satellite-image analysis, comprehensive supply-chain modelling, live utility-reliability data, and production-grade credit-risk modelling are outside the initial scope unless time and reliable data permit.

## 8. Responsible Use and Limitations

Climora is a hackathon prototype. Its scoring formula and thresholds will be explicitly identified as assumptions rather than scientifically validated financial metrics.

The score will not represent a validated probability of default or formal credit rating. It must not be used as the sole basis for approving or rejecting a loan.

The platform is intended to support human review, climate preparedness, and early intervention. Data limitations and missing indicators must be clearly communicated.
