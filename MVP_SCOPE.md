
# Climora — MVP Scope

## 1. Goal

Build a prototype that helps lenders assess climate-related risks affecting Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).

## 2. Target Users

* **Primary:** Bank credit officers and risk analysts.
* **Secondary:** MSME owners who want to understand their climate exposure.

## 3. Core Features (Must Have)

1. **MSME Profile:** Enter business name, business type, location, and basic financial information.
2. **Climate Data:** Retrieve available weather indicators for the selected location.
3. **Risk Assessment:** Generate an illustrative climate-risk score using transparent, rule-based logic.
4. **Risk Breakdown:** Show contributing factors such as heavy rainfall, heat exposure, and flood-related exposure where data is available.
5. **Recommendations:** Suggest practical steps to reduce identified risks.
6. **Dashboard:** Display the score, risk category, contributing factors, and recommendations.

## 4. Optional Features (If Time Permits)

* Interactive map showing business locations.
* Compare risk profiles across multiple MSMEs.
* Download a simple risk report.
* Simulated portfolio-level risk dashboard for lenders.

## 5. Out of Scope

* Production-grade credit scoring.
* Predicting actual loan defaults.
* Direct integration with banking systems.
* Automatic loan approvals or rejections.
* Claims of validated climate-risk predictions.

## 6. Constraints

* Hackathon duration: 24 hours.
* Team size: 3 members.
* Use public or sample data.
* Prioritize a working end-to-end demonstration over a large feature set.

## 7. Responsible Use

The score is an illustrative prototype assessment, not a validated credit rating or probability of default. Data limitations must be disclosed, and the score must not be used as the sole basis for lending decisions.
