  - URL: https://journals.plos.org/climate/article?id=10.1371/journal.pclm.0000644

## Weather APIs

### Primary Provider

- **Open-Meteo** — Free weather and climate APIs (CC BY 4.0). Includes Forecast, Historical, Flood, Climate, and Geocoding APIs. No API key required for non-commercial use.
  - Website: https://open-meteo.com/
  - Forecast API: https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast
  - Historical Weather API: https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive
  - Flood API: https://flood-api.open-meteo.com/v1/flood
  - Climate API: https://climate-api.open-meteo.com/v1/climate
  - Geocoding API: https://geocoding-api.open-meteo.com/v1/search

### Alternative Providers

- **Visual Crossing** — Weather API for historical data, current conditions, and forecasts. Free tier: 1,000 records/day.
  - URL: https://www.visualcrossing.com/weather-data/
- **Meteostat** — Open platform providing free access to historical weather and climate data via JSON API.
  - URL: https://dev.meteostat.net/

## Climate & Flood Datasets

- **World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal** — Historical climate information and climate projections (country/region level). Downloaded datasets require preprocessing.
  - URL: https://climateknowledgeportal.worldbank.org/download-data

- **World Bank Global Flood Exposure Dataset** — Gridded population flood exposure headcounts by country. Requires preprocessing and geographic matching.
  - URL: https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0062763/global-flood-exposure-gridded-exposure-headcounts-bycountry

- **HDX Flood Datasets** — Flood hazard maps and exposure datasets from humanitarian sources. Requires preprocessing.
  - URL: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/?vocab_Topics=flooding
